Everything a book needs. Nothing it doesn't.

JustWrite keeps the tools out of sight until you want them, so the page is all you see while you write.

Just the page

A centred page with a comfortable line length. Light, Sepia and Dark themes, 20 book fonts, and Focus mode hides everything but your words.

Books and chapters

Keep every book in one place. Drag chapters into order, mark them In progress, Draft or Final, and see word counts for each chapter and the whole book.

Versions

Try a chapter another way without losing the first. Make a new version when you want one, switch between them, and lock the ones you want to keep exactly as they are.

Writing goals

Set a target in words or writing time, per day, week or month, and watch the gauge fill. Keep your streak going and give each book a length to aim for.

Ready for print

Export to PDF, Word, EPUB, RTF and text. For KDP and IngramSpark, make a print interior at your trim size and a full cover PDF sized for your page count.

Advance reader copies

Make a personal EPUB or PDF for each early reader. Every copy is invisibly marked, so if one turns up where it shouldn't, JustWrite can tell you whose it was.

Spell check

English (UK and US) built in and 14 more languages to download. Tell it to ignore your characters' names in one book without adding them to every book.

Writing prompts

Stuck for a start? The New Book window offers ideas to begin from: 165 original prompts in 11 genres.

Pictures and covers

Drop pictures into chapters, add your cover and book details, and they go into every export, with the blurb on the back of the printed cover.

Saved as you type

JustWrite saves all the time, and a crash can't leave a half-written chapter. Deleted chapters go to the Recycle Bin, not into thin air.

Your books stay yours

Books are ordinary files on your computer. Keep them where you like, even in Dropbox or OneDrive. No account needed to write.

Updates included

JustWrite updates itself. A JustWrite 2 licence covers every 2.x update, at no extra cost.
